# Schematics

Mohammed Al Rasbi | Team 105 | Interpretation board

The Nano receives distance data over UART and controls the speaker amplifier and vibration driver. The power section supplies 5 V to the controller and drivers.

## Grouped schematic

This one-page layout groups the power supply, Nano controller, data and expansion connectors, audio output, vibration output, and indicators into separate sections. Matching labels connect the sections electrically.
