    Component Selection

    Mohammed Al Rasbi - Interpretation Board

    Prices checked October 21, 2026 (USD, quantity one; tax and shipping excluded).

    Audio amplifier

    Solution Pros Cons
    Option 1: Adafruit 2130 - PAM8302A module

    $3.95 each
    Product link    		 - One channel matches the required warning speaker.

    - Small module and input trim simplify assembly and level adjustment.    		 - Needs analog audio conditioning from PWM.

    - Available output can exceed the selected speaker rating; limit drive.
    Option 2: Adafruit 987 - MAX98306 module

    $8.95 each
    Product link    		 - Selectable gain provides useful volume headroom.

    - Two channels allow a later left/right audio experiment.    		 - Second channel is unnecessary in this design.

    - Higher price than the mono option; speaker power still needs limiting.
    Option 3: Adafruit 1552 - TPA2012 module

    $9.95 each
    Product link    		 - Gain switches simplify prototype tuning.

    - Built-in protection and analog inputs suit a breadboard prototype.    		 - Costs more than the selected mono module.

    - Unused stereo channel and short-speaker-wire guidance complicate packaging.

    Choice: Adafruit 2130 / PAM8302A

    Rationale: The PAM8302A is compact, inexpensive, and provides the single audio channel needed for warning tones. Its volume trim makes adjustment easier; filter and attenuate the PIC PWM signal and keep output within the speaker rating.

    Product photos and specifications: linked supplier pages.

    Speaker

    Solution Pros Cons
    Option 1: Adafruit 3923 - mini oval

    $1.95 each
    Product link    		 - Compact shape fits a handheld body.

    - 1 W rating offers more margin than the mini metal option.    		 - Supplier page currently says out of stock.

    - Short lead and enclosure opening require mechanical planning.
    Option 2: Adafruit 1890 - mini metal

    $1.95 each
    Product link    		 - Small, shallow package is easy to place.

    - Attached wires simplify prototype connections.    		 - Lower continuous rating requires stricter volume limiting.

    - Listed out of stock; title and detailed power ratings differ, so use 0.25 W rated.
    Option 3: Adafruit 1313 - 3-inch speaker

    $1.95 each
    Product link    		 - Mounting tabs provide a secure attachment method.

    - Compatible impedance and power class for the mono amplifier.    		 - Large diameter and depth consume handheld space.

    - Heavier than the oval option and currently listed out of stock.

    Choice: Adafruit 3923 mini oval speaker

    Rationale: The oval speaker fits the handheld enclosure better than the larger round option and has a higher power rating than the mini metal speaker. It is the preferred design choice, but its listed stock shortage must be resolved before ordering.

    Product photos and specifications: linked supplier pages.

    Vibration motor driver

    Solution Pros Cons
    Option 1: Adafruit 2305 - DRV2605L

    $7.95 each
    Product link    		 - Built-in effects help distinguish warning states.

    - Two I2C signals reduce timing work in the PIC.    		 - Requires register initialization and motor calibration/settings.

    - Costs more than the generic H-bridge and changes the earlier PWM interface.
    Option 2: Adafruit 3297 - DRV8833

    $5.95 each
    Product link    		 - Low-voltage operation suits a roughly 3 V motor.

    - Protection and current-limiting capability simplify experiments.    		 - No built-in haptic effect sequencing.

    - Second bridge is unused; default current limit is far above this small motor load.
    Option 3: Adafruit 2448 - TB6612

    $6.95 each
    Product link    		 - Separate motor and logic rails can suit larger actuators.

    - Supports direction and speed control with standard digital signals.    		 - Supplier recommends 4.5-13.5 V motors; poor fit for the selected 3 V ERM.

    - Extra control pins and unused bridge add complexity; rejected for this design.

    Choice: Adafruit 2305 / TI DRV2605L

    Rationale: The DRV2605L provides built-in vibration effects and reduces the software needed to create distinct alerts. Use I2C instead of the earlier PWM-only interface and configure the driver for the selected ERM motor.

    Product photos and specifications: linked supplier pages.

    Vibration motor

    Solution Pros Cons
    Option 1: Adafruit 1201 - vibrating disc

    $1.95 each
    Product link    		 - Enclosed mechanism avoids exposed rotating mass.

    - Low price and compact disc suit grip mounting.    		 - Requires a driver and controlled motor voltage.

    - Mounting stiffness strongly affects feedback; supplier sheet/model consistency needs checking.
    Option 2: Pololu 1637 - shaftless 8 mm

    $6.95 each
    Product link    		 - Smaller diameter fits a narrow grip.

    - Adhesive backing and specified startup current aid integration.    		 - Higher price than the selected disc.

    - Supplier shows backorder availability; smaller force needs grip testing.
    Option 3: Pololu 2265 - cylindrical ERM

    $6.95 each
    Product link    		 - Flat-sided sleeve helps secure the motor.

    - Narrow profile can fit along the grip wall.    		 - Exposed eccentric mass needs clearance.

    - Backorder availability and higher price make it less attractive.

    Choice: Adafruit 1201 vibrating disc

    Rationale: The enclosed disc is small, inexpensive, and easy to mount inside the grip. Drive it through the DRV2605L at a nominal 3 V motor setting, then test the strength of the vibration in the enclosure.

    Product photos and specifications: linked supplier pages.

    Local power regulation

    Solution Pros Cons
    Option 1: Adafruit 2164 - 7805 TO-220

    $0.75 each
    Product link    		 - Through-hole package is practical for a custom student PCB.

    - Low unit cost and fixed output avoid adjustment errors.    		 - Needs input headroom, capacitors and thermal design.

    - Heat reduces battery efficiency; verify actual manufacturer/marking before purchase.
    Option 2: Adafruit 184 - MIC2941 kit

    $14.95 each
    Product link    		 - Lower dropout increases input-voltage flexibility.

    - Kit includes heatsink and prototyping connections.    		 - Large assembled kit and higher cost hurt handheld integration.

    - Listed out of stock; linear heat loss remains despite lower dropout.
    Option 3: Pololu 5433 - D45V5F5

    $5.75 each
    Product link    		 - Switching conversion can reduce battery losses.

    - Very small board with simple VIN/GND/VOUT connections.    		 - Switching-only solution does not meet the stated linear-regulator direction.

    - Current capability depends on input and temperature; rejected as sole local regulator.

    Choice: 7805-based local linear regulator circuit

    Rationale: The 7805 is inexpensive, easy to solder, and supports the required local linear-regulator approach. This choice depends on Jose supplying at least 7 V under load; confirm that input and calculate heatsink requirements before building.

    Product photos and specifications: linked supplier pages.
